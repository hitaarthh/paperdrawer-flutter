# VaultScreen

The iPhone Vault, composed from the system: glass on top, paper in the middle, the dark room behind.

- Large title with a mono eyebrow (`25 ITEMS · ON THIS IPHONE`), round glass Search and Account at the top right.
- FilterBar, then ItemCards sorted by nearest date, `space-3` apart.
- The DeadlineAccessory and the TabBar float over the list; the list scrolls under them with a bottom inset of the measured bar height plus `space-12`.
- On scroll the large title collapses to `nav-title` and the TabBar minimises.
